---
title: Refund policy
access: public
---
An order can be refunded when it belongs to the account this session is logged in to and its
status is "delivered" or "cancelled". The amount refunded is the amount of the order; a part of it
is never refunded. An order that has already been refunded is not refunded again. An order that is
still on the way is refunded only after it has arrived. A refund reaches the account within five
working days; the customer can read the policy at https://halcyon.example/refunds.
